Academic profile

Education

  • PhD, 2009, Universidad Miguel Hernández de Elche. Research on geostatistical models for bioclimatic indices, supervised by Javier Morales and Antonio López-Quílez.
  • Licenciatura in Statistical Sciences and Techniques, 2000, Universidad Miguel Hernández de Elche.
  • Diplomatura in Statistics, 1996, Universidad de Alicante.

Teaching and supervision

University teaching since 2005 across statistics, econometrics, Bayesian modelling, data analysis, time series and experimental design, at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Teaching has included programmes at UMH and Universitat de València.

Supervision of doctoral, master’s and undergraduate research in statistical modelling, environmental analysis, health sciences, and efficiency and productivity. Current research supervision includes Bayesian diagnostic modelling and spatio-temporal environmental analysis.

Teaching recognition includes Talento Docente awards and Excellent evaluations in the DOCENTIA-UMH programme.

Research projects and knowledge transfer

Participation in regional, national and European research projects, including climate-risk modelling in aquaculture through MODESTA–ThinkInAzul, Bayesian environmental modelling, and health research. Applied work connects hospitals, public institutions and industrial partners.

Knowledge-transfer activities include statistical and machine-learning consulting, the eat R package, and training public administrations in generative AI, prompt engineering and responsible use of large language models.

More about Artificial Intelligence research and knowledge transfer.

Scientific leadership

Chair of the Representative Council of the International Biometric Society, 2026–2027, and head of the UMH–FISABIO Joint Research Unit on Statistical Methods in Health Sciences. Deputy Vice-Rector for Research at UMH from 2011 to 2023.

Editorial and peer-review activity includes statistical editing for Public Health Nutrition and reviewing in statistics, ecology and health sciences.
