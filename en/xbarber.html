How a collaboration takes shape

  1. Define the decision, objectives and people who will use the results.
  2. Review the data and design the study or analysis plan.
  3. Develop and validate methods, explaining their results and limitations.
  4. Deliver a report, tool or training that makes the work usable.

A concrete starting point

For an initial conversation, it helps to describe the problem, the data available and the outcome you need. We can then consider the scope and the most suitable form of collaboration.
